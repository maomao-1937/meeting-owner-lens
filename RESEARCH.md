# 2026-10-30 selection notes

Target user: a meeting organizer reviewing an AI-drafted action list before circulation. Frequency is plausibly per meeting, not measured. Inputs are a speaker-labeled transcript, roster declared by the user, and existing action CSV. Output is an evidence-linked queue, never automatic assignment.

Direct original sources: [PM describing extensive note verification, 2026-08-27](https://www.reddit.com/r/projectmanagement/comments/1w0383c/please_tell_me_honestly_how_long_it_takes_you_to/); [different user describing invented action owners, 2026-09-20](https://www.reddit.com/r/MyAIAssistant/comments/1wli9fz/my_meetingnotes_ai_invented_action_owners_who/); [minutes maintainer's transcript/summary drift issue, 2026-07-23](https://github.com/silverstein/minutes/issues/523); [HN discussion of mistaken meeting records and clear owners, 2026-02](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47061194). The Reddit thread's vendor comments were not treated as independent demand. These sources cover months and three channel classes. They cannot prove users want this workflow, that similarity is reliable, or that the product will gain attention.

Alternatives checked before coding: [Fluent](https://becomefluent.io/tools/meeting-action-items/) (free, cloud-provider processing, source lines, rate limits), [Orblex](https://orblex.app/tools/meeting-notes-to-action-items) (free claimed but live form says unavailable), [silverstein/minutes](https://github.com/silverstein/minutes) (integrated local-first meeting product). They may be adequate; the proposed distinction is post-hoc audit of any existing draft with an explicit declared roster and local inference. The resulting candidate alignment is only a reason to inspect the original recording, not verification of a commitment. The source pages offered no credible independent comparative accuracy data, so none is claimed.

Reference repos read 2026-10-30: [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins) for a specific knowledge-work user job; [msitarzewski/agency-agents](https://github.com/msitarzewski/agency-agents) for product and reality-check lenses; newly found [silverstein/minutes](https://github.com/silverstein/minutes) for concrete meeting workflow and counterexample. No third-party code, prompts or datasets were copied. Example text is fictional.
